How To Borrow Books, Keep Them Safe & Return On Time • v1.87

GIVE THIS PAGE TO EVERY LEARNER — KEEP AT HOME

🎓 1. Your Learner Card & Barcode

Every learner gets a card with a barcode like: LRN-G10-0001 — This is YOUR barcode. Do not lose your card. Do not swap cards with a friend. When you borrow a book, the librarian will scan YOUR card + the book. If you lose your card, tell your teacher immediately — they will print a new sticker for you.

📖 2. How To Borrow A Book

1. Bring your Learner Card to the library.
2. Choose the book you need for your subject.
3. Librarian scans your card + book.
4. You get a Slip — Check: Your name, Book title, Due Date.
5. Sign the slip. Keep the slip safe until you return the book.

Rule: You can only have 1 book per subject at a time. Example: If you already have Accounting, you cannot borrow another Accounting until you return the first one. You CAN borrow Accounting + Maths + English at the same time — different subjects is OK.

📥 3. How To Return A Book

1. Bring the book + your slip to the library on or before the Due Date.
2. Librarian scans the book and checks condition.
3. You get a Return Slip — Keep it. This proves you returned it.
4. If you want another book of the same subject, you can borrow it now.

Do NOT give your book to a friend to return for you. Return it yourself with your card.

⏰ 4. Due Date — Return On Time! Your slip shows a Due Date (e.g. 14 days). Return the book on or before that date. If you are late, your name goes on the Overdue List and the Principal will be told. If you are sick or need more time, tell the librarian BEFORE the Due Date — they may extend it. Do not wait until you are overdue.

🛡️ 5. How To Keep Your Book Safe

✅ Cover your book with plastic / brown paper on Day 1.   ✅ Keep it in your school bag, not loose.   ✅ Keep it dry — away from water, rain, food.   ✅ Write your name ONLY where teacher says.   ✅ Keep it away from younger brothers / sisters / pets.
❌ Do NOT tear pages. ❌ Do NOT write / draw in the book. ❌ Do NOT fold pages / break the spine. ❌ Do NOT leave it outside, on the ground, or in the taxi.

⚠️ 6. If Book Is Damaged
Tell the librarian immediately — do not try to fix it with sellotape / glue yourself. If it is a small tear, librarian will fix it. If you hide damage, it will look like you did it later. Be honest — it is better to report early.
❌ 7. If Book Is Lost
Tell your teacher / librarian on the same day you notice it is lost. Tell your parent / guardian. The school will mark it as LOST. Your parent may need to pay / replace it — Principal will decide. Do NOT borrow another book of the same subject until lost book is sorted.
👨‍👩‍👧 For Parents / Guardians: Please help your child: 1) Check that the book is covered and kept safe at home. 2) Check the Due Date on the slip — remind your child to return on time. 3) If book is lost / damaged, please come to school and speak to the Principal / Librarian. 4) Do not buy a photocopied book — the school needs the original barcode book back. 5) If your child says they lost their Learner Card, please tell the school quickly.
