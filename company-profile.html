🎯 About Us

Xintimani Group Holdings (PTY)Ltd builds practical, simple systems for schools and communities. Our flagship product Makhanani LTSM 2.0 helps schools track every textbook — from request, to approval, to issue, to return — so no book is lost and every learner is accounted for.

Vision: Building Solutions. Empowering Communities.
Mission: Give every school a clear, honest, paper-free way to manage LTSM.

📚 What Makhanani LTSM 2.0 Does

  • Teacher requests books for their class
  • HOD approves MAX — only HOD can approve / increase MAX — and can issue for their department only
  • Teacher + HOD + Librarian issue books to learners — shared MAX counter
  • Librarian controls stock, barcode labels, bulk upload of learners / books
  • Principal views whole school — view-only
  • Super Admin views all schools, manages roles + subscriptions — issue view-only
  • Locked rules: MAX block, return does NOT free slot, Book No must be unique, Teacher only own class, HOD only own department

⭐ Why Schools Choose Us

  • ✅ No lost books — every Book No tracked
  • ✅ MAX control stops over-issuing
  • ✅ Works on phone — no computer needed
  • ✅ Barcode labels for fast issue / return
  • ✅ Bulk upload learners + books in minutes
  • ✅ Principal sees whole school at a glance
  • ✅ Audit trail — who issued what, to whom, when
