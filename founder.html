3. Sales Process — From Lead To Paid

Step 1 — Lead: Principal hears about you / You visit school / WhatsApp / Circuit meeting. Send marketing.html link + "Try DEMO 7 days free".
Step 2 — Demo: Principal registers in index.html → Gets DEMO 7 days. Or you create Demo in schools.html → Add School → DEMO.
Step 3 — Day 2: WhatsApp: "How is demo going? Did you add 1 book + 1 learner?" If no, offer 10-min call.
Step 4 — Day 5: WhatsApp: "Demo ends in 2 days. Which plan? STANDARD Yearly is most popular — R12 000 first year."
Step 5 — Day 7: If paid → Approve in schools.html → Click ✅ STANDARD Yearly → Send WhatsApp "✅ Payment received — Active until [date]". If not paid → Login blocks automatically. WhatsApp: "Your demo expired. Pay to reactivate."
Step 6 — Training: Book 1-day training. Use training.html checklist. Get Principal + Librarian + 2 Teachers in room. Use real books.
Step 7 — After Training: WhatsApp Principal next Monday: "How is % Retrieval? Any problems?"
Sales Scripts — Copy-Paste To WhatsApp:

First message:
Hello Principal, I am Vusi from Xintimani Group — Makhanani LTSM. We help schools stop losing books. 7 days free demo, no payment. Can I send you link?

Hello Principal, your demo ends in 2 days. Most schools choose STANDARD — Setup R4 500 + R7 500/year = R12 000 first year. That is less than 40 lost books. Shall I send payment details?

Hello Principal, your demo expired. Your data is safe. Pay to reactivate — STANDARD Yearly R12 000 or Monthly R4 500 + R500/mo. Reply YES and I will send details.

Renewal (7 days before expiry):
Hello Principal, your subscription ends in 7 days ([date]). Renew: STANDARD Yearly R7 500 (no setup again, only yearly fee). Reply YES to renew.

6. Money — How To Collect + Track + Renewals

  • Payment methods: EFT (bank), Cash (receipt), SnapScan / Yoco (if you have), Capitec Pay? — Pick 2, tell all schools same.
  • Proof: Principal sends proof on WhatsApp → You check bank → Approve in schools.html → Send "✅ Payment received" WhatsApp.
  • Track: In schools.html — Last Payment Date + Amount shows per school. Also keep Excel / Notebook: School | Plan | Setup Paid? | Expiry | Amount Paid | Date.

7. Growth — How To Get 20 Schools

  1. 1st school: Your best school — Make it perfect, get testimonial + photos + % Retrieval screenshot.
  2. Circuit meeting: Ask Principal to show Makhanani to other Principals. Offer Principal R500 referral if new school signs? (Your decision)
  3. WhatsApp groups: Principals groups — Post marketing.html + Demo offer.
  4. SGB: Send Principal WhatsApp Report (Principal → Report → WhatsApp) — SGB sees % Retrieval, wants it for their school.
  5. End of year: Best time — Schools lose books in Nov/Dec, panic in Jan. Sell in Nov–Jan.
  6. Don't: Give free forever. Demo 7 days only. If school uses free for 30 days, they will never pay.
Referral Script:
Hello Principal, if you know another Principal who loses books, send them my number. If they sign STANDARD, I will give you R500 off your next renewal. Thank you!
