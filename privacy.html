3. Why We Collect It — Lawful Purpose (POPIA Section 11)

  • 3.1 To provide the service: Let schools manage stock, issue books, and track returns.
  • 3.2 To manage accounts: Login, assign roles (Teacher / HOD / Librarian / Principal), keep system secure.
  • 3.3 To manage subscriptions: Know which school is paid, when renewal is due (30 Nov).
  • 3.4 To comply with law: Keep school LTSM records as required by school / DBE policy.
  • 3.5 With your consent: Where required, we rely on your consent. You may withdraw consent by emailing us, but we may then not be able to provide the service.

4. Who Sees Your Information?

  • 4.1 Your School Only: Teachers, HOD, Librarian, Principal and School Admin in YOUR school can see learner + stock data for YOUR school.
  • 4.2 Super Admin: Xintimani Super Admin can see all schools to provide support and fix accounts.
  • 4.3 We do NOT: Sell, rent, or share your personal information to advertisers or third parties for marketing.
  • 4.4 Service Providers: Data is stored on Firebase / Google Cloud. Google acts as our Operator and stores data securely. See Google Privacy Policy for their handling.
  • 4.5 Law: We will share information only if required by South African law, court order, or to protect safety.

5. How We Protect Your Information (POPIA Section 19)

  • 5.1 Login Protection: Firebase Authentication — passwords are encrypted, never stored as plain text.
  • 5.2 Database Rules: Only logged-in users can read / write. No public access to personal data.
  • 5.3 Role Access: Teacher cannot edit stock. Learner cannot see other learners' data beyond what school allows. Super Admin access is limited to authorised staff.
  • 5.4 HTTPS: All pages use secure HTTPS.
  • 5.5 No System Is 100% Secure: We take reasonable steps, but we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you suspect a breach, email us immediately at vusichauke3037@gmail.com.
