1. The Pitch — 30 Seconds

Say this, word-for-word, to every Principal:

"Hello Principal, I'm Vusi Chauke from Xintimani Group. I built Makhanani LTSM — A system that stops lost books.

Right now, if a learner loses a textbook, you lose R300-R500. If you lose 2 books a month, you already lose R1 000.

Makhanani costs R500/month. Issue a book in 3 seconds, see who has which book, WhatsApp parent in 1 click if overdue.

Can I show you in 10 minutes? Free demo, no obligation."

Rules:

  • Do NOT start with price. Start with lost books pain.
  • Do NOT talk about tech. Talk about money saved.
  • Do NOT ask "Do you want it?" Ask "Can I demo 10 min this week?"
If Principal says "We use paper": "Paper gets lost. Paper doesn't WhatsApp parent. Makhanani shows you in 3 seconds who has which book, even if Librarian is sick."
