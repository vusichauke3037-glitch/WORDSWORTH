"Please try it""Try it with 5 learners for 7 days free. If you don't love it, no payment."
"I need money""I set you up today, your Librarian starts tomorrow."
"Discount?""I already included free training. I can give 7 days free trial, not discount."

⭐ Social Proof — Use After First 3 Schools

Once you have 3 schools, use their names (with permission):

"WORDSWORTH, THUTO, and [School 3] are already using Makhanani. They issue books in 3 seconds. Would you like to see how they do it?"

Ask first happy Principal:

"Principal, can I mention your school name when I talk to other Principals? It helps other schools trust Makhanani."

Most will say YES. That is your best marketing.

🚫 What NOT To Do

Don'tWhy
Don't build custom features for 1 school for freeYou will spend 10 hours for R500. Say: "I will add it to the list for all schools in next update."
Don't give free setup to "big" schoolIf they can't pay setup, they won't pay monthly.
Don't argue with Principal on WhatsAppSay: "Let me come show you live, 10 minutes." Face-to-face closes, WhatsApp argues.
Don't post prices on Facebook publiclyPrincipals negotiate. Keep prices in WhatsApp / SGB Letter only.
Don't promise Department approvalSay: "This is a school management tool, like a register. It helps you manage LTSM."
