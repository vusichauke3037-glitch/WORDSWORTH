ℹ️ About Makhanani LTSM

Makhanani means “to remember / to keep in mind” — we remember every book, every learner.

Rules:
1. Grade must match — Grade 10 book → Grade 10 learner ONLY.
2. Same Title Block — Learner cannot have same Title OUT twice.
3. Teacher = Own Subject ONLY.
4. Subscription must be ACTIVE — Suspended / Expired = Cannot login (except Admin).
5. Due Date: 30 November — All books must be returned.
