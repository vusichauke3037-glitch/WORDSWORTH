📋 Bulk CSV — Add Many Learners At Once

CSV Format — 4 columns, first row = header:
Name,Grade,Class,LearnerID
Example:
Name,Grade,Class,LearnerID
Thabo Mokoena,Grade 10,10A,2024001
Naledi Dube,Grade 10,10A,2024002

Grade must be exactly like Grade 10 (with space + capital G). Class like 10A. LearnerID must be unique.
