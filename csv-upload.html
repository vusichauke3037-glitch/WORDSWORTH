ℹ️ How To Make CSV In Excel

1. Open Excel → Type headers in Row 1 exactly as in Template.
2. Fill rows below. No empty rows in middle.
3. Save As → CSV UTF-8 (Comma delimited) (*.csv)
4. Come back here → Choose File → Preview → Upload.
5. Grade: You can type 10 or Grade 10 — App fixes to Grade 10.
6. Subject: You can type maths — App fixes to MATHEMATICS.
7. Duplicates: If barcode / email already exists, that row is SKIPPED, not duplicated.
