⚡ Quick Rules

  • Textbook Number = School Initials + 6 Digits. Example: Makhanani Primary School = MPS000001.
  • 1 copy = 1 number. 5 copies of same title = 5 different numbers — MPS000001 to MPS000005.
  • Lost label? Click 📚 Print Barcode on same textbook → Same number → Print again. Do NOT make new number.
  • Stick inside front cover + Cover with clear tape.
