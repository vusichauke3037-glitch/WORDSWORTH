💰 3. Financial Projection — Admin Only — v1.0

🔒 FINANCIAL PROJECTION IS ADMIN ONLY. Even Principal cannot see it. Principal sees pricing on Home / Principal, but NOT revenue / projections, NOT this checklist, NOT Audit.
Open from: Admin → 💰 OPEN Financial Projection OR direct: financial-projection.html (gated)
What it shows (live from Firebase):
• Headline: Active Schools, MRR, Next 12 Months Revenue, 3-Year Revenue
• Year-by-Year: Year 1 / 2 / 3 — Setup / Monthly / Once-off / Total
• By Plan: DEMO / STARTER / STANDARD / PREMIUM — count, Setup, Monthly, Once-off, Year 1
• Per School: What each school pays, Billing, Status, Setup Paid / Not Paid, Next Renewal / End, Year 1 Revenue
• What-If Calculator: “If we add X schools on STANDARD Once-off” → Extra Year 1 / 2 / 3 + Combined 3-Year
Rule in ProjectionHow It Works
Setup Once-off for LifeSetup counted ONCE per school. Year 1 Setup = only for schools that have NOT paid Setup yet. Year 2 / 3 Setup = R0 — Already Paid. Existing schools = treated as Setup Already Paid.
Suspended / ExpiredExcluded from projection — R0 until reactivated. Reactivate in Admin → Total goes back up.
DEMO / TRIALR0 — counted in school count, not in revenue.
Monthly schoolsPay every month: Year 1 = Monthly x 12, Year 2 = Monthly x 12, Year 3 = Monthly x 12. No churn assumed.
Once-off schoolsPay once per year: Year 1 = Once-off x 1, Year 2 = Once-off x 1, Year 3 = Once-off x 1. No churn assumed.
What-IfDoes NOT save to Firebase — on-screen only. New schools pay Setup ONCE in Year 1, then Renewal only in Year 2 / 3.
💚 Worked Example — What-If: 10 new STANDARD Once-off schools
Year 1 extra = 10 x (R4 500 Setup + R7 500 Once-off) = R120 000
Year 2 extra = 10 x R7 500 = R75 000 (No Setup)
Year 3 extra = 10 x R7 500 = R75 000 (No Setup)
3-Year extra = R270 000
