⚠️ Assumptions + Notes — Read Before Sharing

Assumptions:
1. Setup = Once-off for Life — counted only if school has NOT paid Setup yet. Existing schools in Firebase are treated as Setup Already Paid (unless Setup Paid = NO in Add School).
2. Suspended schools = R0 until reactivated — they are excluded from MRR / Year totals.
3. DEMO / TRIAL = R0.
4. Monthly schools pay every month for 12 months in Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 — no churn assumed.
5. Once-off schools pay once per year — Year 1, Year 2, Year 3 — no churn assumed.
6. No new schools in “Current Schools” totals — use What-If calculator for growth.
