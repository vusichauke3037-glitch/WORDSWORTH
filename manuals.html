9. BULK UPLOAD — Bulk Learner + Bulk Book — CSV Only — 500 At Once

9.1 Why Bulk Upload?

Without Bulk: 500 learners x 1 minute each = 500 minutes = 8 hours — Teacher types all day, makes typos.
With Bulk: Excel list → Save As CSV → Upload → Preview → IMPORT → 500 learners in 2 minutes — IDs auto, no typos, no duplicates.
Same for books: 500 books x 1 minute = 8 hours → Bulk: 2 minutes.
